Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit the Sarah Miller digital cookbook website, contact us, or purchase our digital recipe products through Whop. We want you to understand what information may be collected, why it is used, and how you can contact us about privacy questions.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website and products, the information we handle may include:

  • Information you provide: Your name, email address, subject, order number (if provided), and the contents of messages submitted through our contact form or sent to us by email.
  • Purchase-related information: Product purchased, order reference, purchase date, order status, and other information made available to us by Whop as necessary to fulfill orders or provide customer support and handle refund requests.
  • Technical information: Providers involved in operating and securing this website may process technical data, such as IP address, browser type, device information, and website access logs.

Our landing page does not ask you to enter payment-card information. Payments are completed using Whop and the payment services available through its checkout.

2. How We Use Information

We use information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Answer questions and respond to requests submitted to customer support.
  • Identify purchases, help with digital cookbook access, and process eligible refund requests.
  • Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the website and contact form.
  • Keep business records and meet applicable legal obligations.
  • Understand website performance only to the extent that analytics tools are actually enabled and appropriately disclosed.

Submitting a support message does not, by itself, sign you up for marketing emails.

3. Checkout and Digital Delivery Through Whop

Purchases are completed through Whop, a separate platform that provides checkout and related payment services. Whop and its payment partners process payment and account information under their own terms and privacy practices. Whop may make relevant buyer and transaction information available to us for order support, product access, and refunds.

Please review Whop’s Privacy Policy for details about how Whop processes information. Our Privacy Policy applies to the information we handle for our own website and customer support; it does not replace Whop’s policy.

4. When Information Is Shared

Information may be made available to service providers involved in operating our website, delivering contact messages, providing digital products, or managing purchases and refunds. These may include the website hosting provider, the contact-form/email delivery provider, and Whop. We may also disclose information if required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website or its hosting provider may use technical cookies or logs needed for functionality, security, and performance. Analytics or advertising tools, if installed, may collect additional information about visits and interactions.

You can manage some cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect site functionality. Where required by law, additional information and choices about tracking technologies will be provided. The separate Whop checkout may use its own cookies and tracking practices, as described in Whop’s policies.

6. How Long Information Is Kept

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for customer support, purchases, refunds, security, recordkeeping, or applicable legal obligations. Retention may vary depending on the type of information and the service provider involved. We will review requests for deletion where required by applicable law, subject to permitted exceptions.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to help protect information handled through the website and customer support. However, no website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. We recommend avoiding the submission of sensitive financial information through our contact form.

8. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may be entitled to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to exercise other rights concerning how it is used. You may contact us using the email below. We may need to verify your request before responding.

For information managed directly by Whop, you may also need to use the privacy options or contact channels identified in Whop’s Privacy Policy.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website may link to third-party services, including Whop. Those services are responsible for their own content and privacy practices. We encourage you to review the privacy notices of any service you visit.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our website, service providers, or legal obligations. When changes are made, the updated version will be posted here with a revised effective date, and additional notice will be provided where required by law.
